THE EVIDENCE IS THE INTERFACE
A school compares nightly sleep (hours) and next-day reaction time (ms) for 28 students. Describe the association, then explain how the highlighted high-leverage observation affects the least-squares regression line.
▦ Accessible data table
Sample response

Across the 28 students, nightly sleep and next-day reaction time have a moderately strong, negative, roughly linear association.

The high-leverage point at 9.1 hours and 338 ms pulls the right side of the least-squares line upward, making the slope less negative; without it, the negative association would appear stronger.

Common loss: “The point changes the line.” This does not explain leverage or direction.
