## Review invariants

- Scoring anchors retain one meaning and never appear as overview/mastery status.
- Assistance progresses from least to most revealing; primary actions do not vend answers.
- The learner sees one calm recommended entry while optional detail remains pull-based.
- No streaks, points-as-progress, leaderboards, paywalls, parent UI, or reward animation.
