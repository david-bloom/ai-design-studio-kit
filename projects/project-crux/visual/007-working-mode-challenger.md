## One-sentence signature

**A blue-hour specimen cabinet where hard ideas appear as light-exposed scientific photograms and the learner's reasoning travels as a single hand-couched thread.**

## Named tactile-craft signature

The net-new signature is **cyanotype photogram + hand-couched thread**: diagram elements look physically arranged on sensitized cloth and exposed to light, leaving pale silhouettes and deep-blue blooms; one real-looking thread is laid over the cloth and visibly tacked down at meaningful causal or inferential steps. This craft appears in none of the six directions. It creates picture-book tactility without illustration characters, fake notebook styling, or scrapbook clutter.

The thread is an interaction trace, never a score. It shifts with controls and shows the learner where a changed variable propagates. It is always mineral cyan `#2BD9C5`, supplemented by a moving dashed contour for reduced-color contexts.

## Fixed three-column architecture

The plates use a `276 / fluid / 292px` grid at 1440×900. The bones remain unchanged.

### Left — Rubric specimen drawer

The rubric is chunked into three collapsible specimen labels. Each chunk carries plain-language evidence, its scoring anchor, and a small **open** control. Learner B sees one expanded criterion and two quiet labels. Learner A can expand all criteria or show rubric wording. Nothing fills like a progress meter; the drawer reports what evidence matters, not points accumulated.

### Center — Exposure table

The question and interactive scientific model sit on one deep Prussian cyanotype cloth. The content diagram is the brightest object in the product. A single Mineral Thread shows how the active manipulation propagates through the model. Controls sit directly beneath the model, followed by a prediction prompt and a compact response surface. The learner must predict, manipulate, then explain.

### Right — Reference negative rack

Persistent glossary material appears as three translucent “negative sleeves”: equations, vocabulary, and one study tip. Each can disclose one additional layer in place. The rack is present but visually quieter than the question. It never becomes an answer panel.

### One bounded optional deviation

The center's Mineral Thread may cross **behind** the two column gutters to visually tack one rubric criterion and one glossary term to the active phenomenon. It never carries content across columns and can be disabled with **Quiet thread**. This is ornamental continuity over the intact three-column architecture, not a structural change.

## Interaction language and help-not-vend

Micro-motion resembles cloth being exposed: a change resolves from deep blue to pale silhouette over 180–240ms; the thread slides and is re-tacked rather than bouncing. Reduce Motion swaps this for an immediate state change plus a short text update.

Assistance remains ordered and explicit:

1. **Nudge** — reveals one question to ask yourself.
2. **Check one link** — checks one causal or inferential connection in the learner's sentence.
3. **Walk the thread** — explains the sequence of relationships without writing the response.
4. **Study a worked solution** — reveals a complete separate example after warning that it replaces productive struggle for this attempt.

No control reveals the current answer directly. Feedback attaches one scoring anchor to evidence already present or asks for one specific revision.

## Cross-subject proof

### AP Statistics plate

The center is a least-squares influence model. Moving a high-leverage observation updates the fitted line, slope, correlation, and residual. The Mineral Thread runs from the moved point through the ghost line to the changing statistics. The learner predicts before moving it and then explains the influence in context. Exact values and a data-table route remain available.

### AP Biology plate

The center is a membrane-transport model, not a reused chart. A phospholipid bilayer is rendered as a cyanotype botanical cross-section; changing external solute concentration updates water-direction arrows and relative cell volume. The Mineral Thread runs from the concentration control through water potential to cell response. The learner predicts the direction of net water movement, manipulates, and explains osmosis without being given the causal sentence.

## Why this could not be mistaken for the synthesis or any of the six

This claim is falsifiable: obscure the title and compare the plate at thumbnail size. The challenger must still be identifiable by **a predominantly Prussian-blue cyanotype field, pale exposure silhouettes, rectilinear specimen drawers, and one visibly tacked mineral thread crossing the scientific model**. The synthesis is described as warm cream with muted violet-coral and soft-serif paper; none of those features appears here. Draw the Door uses hand-drawn routes on canvas but not light-exposed cloth or sewn causal traces; Soft Landing uses floating translucent cards; Pocket Universe uses a jewel-dark zoomable field; Lantern Fold uses parchment folios; Say It Bright uses poster typography; Three Guide Registers uses characters. If the four listed Blue Hour features are removed or if the plate reads primarily as any of those six devices, the distinctiveness claim has failed.

## What the challenger recovers

The incumbent proved that rubric, model, and glossary belong together, but its warm-generic surface did not create a place with memory. The Blue Hour Cabinet gives those same utilities a specific soul: the hush of making a scientific photogram after dusk, the slight irregularity of cloth and thread, and the reassuring idea that a difficult relationship can appear gradually when exposed to attention. It restores children's-literature wonder through authored craft rather than age-coded decoration, while giving the product a signature that can be named, recognized, and tested head-to-head.
