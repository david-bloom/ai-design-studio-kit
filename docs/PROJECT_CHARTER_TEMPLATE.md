## Expanded understanding

What the Creative Director's brief-development conversation surfaced that the origin brief didn't say outright:

- **Real audience:** who is this actually for, and how do we know?
- **Real constraints vs. assumed ones:** what's actually fixed (budget, tech stack, legal/regulatory, timeline) vs. what was just how the brief happened to be phrased?
- **Unstated goals:** what does success look like that the brief didn't articulate?
- **Reference/competitive context:** what exists already that this needs to differentiate from, or can learn from?
- **Open questions:** anything still unresolved that a later stage needs to close before proceeding.
